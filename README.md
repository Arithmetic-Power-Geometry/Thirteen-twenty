# Thirteen–Twenty: Additive-Frequency Research

This repository contains mathematical results, exact computational certificates, scheduling implementations, and reproducibility resources for additive frequency triples `(a, b, a+b)`.

## Research publications and supporting resources

### 1. Sharp Safe-Time Measures for Additive Frequency Triples

**Title:** *Sharp Safe-Time Measures for Additive Frequency Triples: Sixteen Certified Sharp Bounds and Rational Threshold Geometry*

**Author:** Mohammad Amir Khusru Akhtar (2026)

**Research and reproducibility artifacts:** [Additive-triple safe-time certificates](reproducibility/additive-triple-safe-time-2026)

**Published research record:** Akhtar, M. A. K. (2026). *Sharp Safe-Time Measures for Additive Frequency Triples: Sixteen Certified Sharp Bounds and Rational Threshold Geometry* (Version V1). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23296016

This work examines safe-time measures of the coupled frequencies `a`, `b`, and `a+b`, establishes sixteen certified sharp lower bounds for reciprocal thresholds `1/q` with `5 <= q <= 20`, and develops exact integer/rational verification methods and threshold geometry. The global sharp-bound formula for all `q >= 5` is not claimed.

### 2. Exact Certificate-Aware Nonpreemptive Scheduling

**Title:** *Exact Certificate-Aware Nonpreemptive Scheduling with General Availability Calendars and Additive-Frequency Bounds*

**Authors:** Mohammad Amir Khusru Akhtar and Anand Nayyar (2026)

**Research implementation and results:** [Real-time scheduling 13/20](real-time-scheduling-13-20)

This work studies nonpreemptive single-machine scheduling on rational availability calendars, including calendars generated by coupled additive frequencies. It develops an exact subset dynamic program, rational feasibility-witness checks, and finite computational validation. The additive-frequency availability theorem yields a guaranteed contiguous safe interval; it does not imply a corresponding multi-job throughput guarantee.

## Repository scope

The two research contributions share additive-frequency geometry but address different questions: the first concerns extremal safe-time measures across reciprocal thresholds; the second concerns admission, scheduling, and verification under availability constraints.

Research sources, numerical results, and reproducibility instructions are maintained in their respective linked directories. These results concern structured additive-frequency triples and are not presented as a resolution of the general Lonely Runner Conjecture.
