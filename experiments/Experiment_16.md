# Experiment 16: Additive triple with comparable summands

**Archive status:** Retrospectively reconstructed from the earlier research conversation on 2026-10-10. This file records the result at that stage, not an independently reviewed discovery.

## Research question and result
At t=1/(2(a+b)), triple a,b,a+b has minimum min(a,b)/(2(a+b)); >=1/16 when max(a,b)<=7 min(a,b).

## Limitations / novelty
Only a constrained witness, not all triples with twelve arbitrary extra speeds.
