# Experiment 15: Prime-modulus shifting

**Archive status:** Retrospectively reconstructed from the earlier research conversation on 2026-10-10. This file records the result at that stage, not an independently reviewed discovery.

## Research question and result
If many speeds are divisible by prime p, shifting t by j/p preserves their phases while restricting the other speeds. Particular p=17 calculation handles special 15-speed classes.

## Limitations / novelty
Related to classical modular shifting; no novelty verified.

## Development chain
The sequence progresses from elementary special configurations, through arithmetic residues and finite candidate-time sets, to a sharp quantitative additive-triple safe-time theorem. Experiment 25 contains the strongest supported version; see [the full report](../docs/RESEARCH_REPORT.md).
