# Experiment 19: Measure and covering reformulation

**Archive status:** Retrospectively reconstructed from the earlier research conversation on 2026-10-10. This file records the result at that stage, not an independently reviewed discovery.

## Research question and result
Define T(a,b) safe times for a,b,a+b at delta=1/16. Union bound gives measure >=5/8. Suggested >=13/20 based on bounded tests.

## Limitations / novelty
The proposed sharp inequality was unproved at this stage.
