# Experiment 18: Counting candidates and obstruction

**Archive status:** Retrospectively reconstructed from the earlier research conversation on 2026-10-10. This file records the result at that stage, not an independently reviewed discovery.

## Research question and result
At t=(2j+1)/(2c), the allowed phases of a lie in [1/16,7/16] union [9/16,15/16]; approximately 3/4 candidates work. Additional speed 2c makes every such t fail.

## Limitations / novelty
Demonstrates why restricting to half-integer c-phase cannot settle general case.
