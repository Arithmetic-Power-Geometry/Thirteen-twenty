## Critical go/no-go

Do not claim Q1-level original CS research if CS-007 yields only standard interval scheduling, standard NP-hardness inherited without an applicable reduction, or a trivial arithmetic corollary. In that case the number of steps cannot be treated as a fixed countdown: a new research question may be needed.

## Research status
