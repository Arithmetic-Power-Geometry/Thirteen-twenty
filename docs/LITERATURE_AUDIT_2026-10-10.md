## Important caveats

- An existing BibTeX entry is not evidence that the corresponding paper is cited in a future manuscript. Citation placement and claim-to-source alignment must be checked when drafting the paper.
- This audit did not establish novelty of any experiment or solve the unrestricted Lonely Runner Conjecture.
- Existing preprint entries and historical citations should be rechecked for final version metadata immediately before submission.
- The experiment INDEX file is stale: it stops at Experiment 48, while the repository contains reports through Experiment 58. Do not rely on INDEX alone to count reports.
