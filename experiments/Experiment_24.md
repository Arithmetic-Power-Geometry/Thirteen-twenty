# Experiment 24: General coprime finite evidence

**Archive status:** Retrospectively reconstructed from the earlier research conversation on 2026-10-10. This file records the result at that stage, not an independently reviewed discovery.

## Research question and result
Scaling reduces to coprime pairs. Earlier search reportedly tested 2,090 pairs with minimum 13/20; elementary universal lower bound 5/8+1/(8 max(a,b)).

## Limitations / novelty
Finite testing is not universal proof.
