## Novelty verdict

The exact safe-window enumerator, linear/binary/segment-tree admission, equal-job packing capacity, interval erosion, certificate checking and fixed-two-window PARTITION reduction use established general techniques. The unusual arithmetic origin of the safe windows alone does not prove novelty. A comprehensive search of availability-constrained nonpreemptive scheduling, forbidden intervals, temporal resource calendars, interval packing, arithmetic dynamics and related combinatorics remains necessary.
