## Priority and novelty implications

Shabtay (2022) explicitly states that single-machine nonpreemptive scheduling with one fixed unavailability interval was already known to be NP-hard, and describes pseudo-polynomial and approximation algorithms. Ma et al. (2010) surveys extensive results on deterministic machine unavailability across machine environments. Thus the manuscript's fixed (1,1,2) two-window PARTITION reduction should be presented as a **specialization/complexity confirmation**, not as a general novelty breakthrough.
